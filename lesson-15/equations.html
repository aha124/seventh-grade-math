The rule: whatever you do to one side, do to the other side. The goal is x alone.

Plus something? Subtract it from both sides. Minus something? Add it to both sides.

A whole number times x? Divide both sides by it. A fraction times x? Multiply both sides by its flip.

Two steps: undo the adding or subtracting first, then the multiplying. Then check: put your answer back in.

Fraction answers: type 5/2 or 2 1/2 (the space key goes between the whole number and the fraction).
